# SPEC-1-LTI Plugin

## Gathering Results

### Success criteria
- **Functional validation**
  - LTI 1.3 launch works consistently across Moodle roles (student, instructor).  
  - Realtime feedback loop functions with <200 ms latency (press → dashboard update).  
  - Tutoring sessions are generated automatically after confusion triggers.  
  - Participation credit is posted correctly to Moodle gradebook (if enabled).  

- **Instructor adoption**
  - ≥70% of pilot instructors report BluNote improved lecture pacing and engagement.  
  - ≥50% reduction in repetitive confusion-related questions during/after class.  

- **Student engagement**
  - ≥60% of students use the “Confused” button at least once during pilot.  
  - ≥75% of confused students open and interact with tutoring content.  
  - Anonymous survey shows ≥70% report increased understanding after tutoring.  

- **Institutional outcomes**
  - Evidence of improved course completion/retention compared to baseline.  
  - Measurable improvement in student satisfaction survey items on “clarity of lectures” and “availability of support”.  
  - Documentation for accreditation: lecture engagement metrics, intervention logs.  

### Data collection methods
- **System logs**: latency metrics, usage counts (button presses, dashboard loads, tutoring sessions).  
- **Instructor dashboards**: export anonymized confusion analytics per lecture.  
- **Surveys**: short post-lecture feedback forms for students and instructors.  
- **Comparative analysis**: compare grades and dropout rates of pilot courses with historical control groups.  

### Continuous improvement loop
1. **Collect** engagement and tutoring data from live sessions.  
2. **Analyze** triggers, tutoring completion, and grade impact.  
3. **Review** with instructors and university admins after each pilot cycle.  
4. **Iterate** on threshold settings, tutoring prompts, and dashboard UX.
